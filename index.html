Rumors persist regarding the recent unrest at the Ministry of Magic, during which He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named was spotted again. "Don't ask us anything; we're forbidden to talk about it," said a very agitated Memory Eraser, who wished to remain anonymous, outside the Ministry last night. However, high-ranking Ministry sources confirm that the fabled Hall of Prophecies was at the center of the unrest. While Ministry officials still refuse to even confirm that such a place exists, a significant portion of the wizarding community is convinced that the Death Eaters currently serving time in Azkaban for trespassing and attempted robbery sought to steal a certain prophecy. No one knows the prophecy's meaning, but many believe it concerns Harry Potter—the only person we know of who survived the Killing Curse and, as far as we know, was present at the Ministry on the night of the riots. Some even refer to Potter as "the Chosen One," believing that, according to the prophecy, he alone can rid us of He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named. The current location of the prophecy, if it actually exists, is unknown.
